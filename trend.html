MM Trend is MarketMiner's own trend indicator: an ATR trailing stop drawn as a green (up) / red (down) band. Buy / Sell when the band flips on a closed candle (signals never redraw), stop at the band, TP1 / TP2 / TP3 at 1R / 2R / 3R, stop to breakeven after TP1. Percentages are over the signals in the loaded history. Analysis only, not advice; no orders are placed.
