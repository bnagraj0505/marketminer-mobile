How AuctionFlow reads the market

Balance: price rotating around its own value (POC crossed at least twice, an hour or more) — buyers and sellers agree; fade the value edges toward the POC, never trade the middle. Imbalance: three closes outside value and beyond the balance range = acceptance; the market travels to the next balance (projected zones), pull-backs to the old edge are the entries. Failed auction: a probe beyond the balance range or yesterday's high/low that closes straight back in value — trade back through value toward the POC. Poor high/low (2+ TPOs at the extreme) and naked POCs tend to be revisited. HVN = price accepted (stalls), LVN = price rejected (moves fast). Index volume comes from the front-month future.
