How to read this page
Top cards. The future's live price, with spot and the basis (future minus spot). Nearest Big Buyers is the closest price bucket below the price where an unusually large number of buy orders are waiting (at least 2.5× the typical bucket, held for 15 seconds or more). Nearest Big Sellers is the same above the price. The spot figure in brackets is the same level translated to the index (level minus basis).
Green / red bar. The share of all waiting buy vs sell orders (for India the exchange's total buy and sell quantity; for crypto the 20 visible levels each side).
Ladder. Orders waiting, grouped per bucket (5 points for NIFTY). Green = buyers waiting, red = sellers waiting, the yellow row is where the price is now. Hatched bars are levels seen earlier that are now outside the visible depth (shown until the price trades through them or for 10 minutes).
Right column. What happened at each bucket in the chosen activity window: bought = buyers took sellers' orders there, sold = sellers hit buyers' orders there, pulled = orders cancelled before they were filled (the orange dots, bigger = more). A wall with a lot pulled and little traded is probably not real support or resistance: spoofing, or algorithms moving their quotes.
Depth. Indian futures: the exchange shows the best 5 prices each side (Angel One live stream, several updates a second; FYERS every 15 s when Angel One is not connected). Crypto: Binance USDⓈ-M futures, 20 prices each side every 100 ms and every trade, so bought/sold there is exact. Big walls further away than the visible depth cannot be seen.
